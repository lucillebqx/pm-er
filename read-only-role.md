### Context
Team managers will be contacted to inform them that their team members will no longer be able to have the full customer support rights on JobCloud ONE. Instead, their roles will be limited to read-only. 
I want to inform the Team Managers of the upcoming changes and let know what will happen when and their available options. 
There are two scenarios 
- scenario 1: read-only role is straightforward for the position
- scenario 2: we need to confirm with the Team Manager before we proceed 


### INSTRUCTIONS 
You will be given a list and user will prompt you to use slack messages. 


SCENARIO 1 - this is the text 

Hi [Name],

I hope you're doing well!

I'm reaching out about a small but important change in the back office of JobCloud One. Today, more than 180 back-office accounts can read and edit customer data (company profiles, Ecom, user management and job ads), even though most colleagues only need to read it. Limiting edit rights to those who really need them will reduce the risk of unintended or unauthorised changes to customer data (and of human error) and support the privacy first vision of JobCloud internal processes. 

This is the first initiative in the Domain Team's delivery board.

What happens next
