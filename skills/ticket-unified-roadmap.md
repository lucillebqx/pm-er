## Format of the tickt template 
1. Basics *

Field

Title

Business owner

Submitted by (if different)

Date submitted

Business unit / brand

 

2. One-line summary *

Complete the sentence. If it is hard to write, the idea needs more thought.

We want to [achieve what] for [which users] so that [business benefit].

3. Problem or opportunity *

What happens today, and why is it a problem or a missed opportunity? Do not mention the solution here. 3–5 sentences.

Good: "SME recruiters abandon job-ad creation at the pricing step." Not: "We need a new pricing page."

4. Who is affected *

Tick all that apply and estimate how many are affected.

Job seekers

Recruiters SE

Recruiters LME

Staffing agencies

Internal teams (eg sales, customer service, operations, …)

Other:

Rough size of the affected group (number or share of segment):

5. Evidence

What makes us believe this is real and worth solving? Name the source. "Gut feeling" is allowed — say so explicitly.

Type

What we know

Source / link

Data / analytics

Customer feedback, quotes, tickets

Sales (lost deals, requests)

Market / competitors

Gut feeling

 

6. Desired outcome and success metric *

What will be measurably different when this is done? Give at least one metric. No baseline? Write "unknown" — measuring it becomes the first task. No number at all? Describe the observable behaviour change instead.

Metric

Today (baseline)

Target

By when

How we measure it

Primary metric

Secondary / guardrail metric (optional)

 

Behaviour change we expect to see (optional): e.g. "Recruiters no longer call customer service to renew ads."

7. Business impact and why now *

Question

Answer

Impact type

Revenue · Retention / churn · Cost / efficiency · Strategic positioning · Legal / compliance

Size of impact

Low · Medium · High — plus one sentence of reasoning, CHF if you can

Deadline or trigger

Contract, regulation, season, competitor launch — or "none"

Cost of doing nothing

What happens if we wait 6 months?

8. Initial solution hypothesis

Optional. Your first idea of how to solve it — a hypothesis, not a spec. Product and engineering may propose alternatives.

9. Out of scope

1–3 bullets on what this item is explicitly not about.

 

10. Appetite

How much is this worth to us? This is about value, not an effort estimate.

Small — a few days

Medium — a few weeks

Large — a quarter or more

11. Risks, dependencies and open questions

Other teams, partners, data, legal / GDPR (candidate data!), and what we don't know yet.

 

12. Confidence

How sure are you that this will deliver the outcome in section 6?

High — strong evidence

Medium — some evidence

Low — mostly a hunch
