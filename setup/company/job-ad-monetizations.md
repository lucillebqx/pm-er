## JobCloud Core Job seekers Platforms
Because Switzerland is deeply divided by language, JobCloud strategically operates different platforms to dominate specific regions.
Instead of running a single website, JobCloud maintains distinct regional portals to match Swiss linguistic demographics: 
- jobs.ch: The number one job platform in German-speaking Switzerland. It covers all industries, from retail to highly specialized technical roles. 
- jobup.ch: The number one job platform in French-speaking Switzerland (Romandie). Its branding and focus are entirely tailored to the western cantons (Geneva, Vaud, Neuchâtel, Jura, and parts of Fribourg and Valais). 
- JobScout24.ch: A highly recognizable secondary broad-market portal that acts as a strong alternative across the country. 
- Niche Portals: They also capture specific segments through targeted sites like Alpha.ch (for executives), topjobs.ch, and financejobs.ch. 

## Monetization models - currently available
JobCloud offers multiple job ad monetization methods to its clients 
- Pay per Duration 
On Jobs, Jobup and JS 24, customers JobCloud offers different advertizing options based on the number of days of publication and other elements such as "boosts" (email reminders sent to job seekers). JobCloud does not offer any guarantee in terms of applications (volume and suitability to the job ad requirements). The ads are posted for the agreed upon number of days on the websites and go offline accordingly. 
This has been the core monetization model. 
It works with credits (one credit = one job ad live). 
Customers can buy credits online, individually and in pack. 
Customers with bigger hiring needs can contact the Sales team who will accomodate them.  
For the recurrent hiring needs, JobCloud offers slots: slots allow customers to purchase a time-boxed subscription (3/6/12 months) for a selected number of concurrently published job ads.
Each job ad is placed into one slot and each slot space can be used multiple times per month through ad rotation.
It is not available for self-service purchase, and it can be added only by customer service teams.

- PRO Programmatic Ad Distribution: For companies advertising roles, JobCloud does not just post a text block. They run programmatic, managed-performance ads that push the vacancy out to specific target demographics across search engines (like Google) and partner networks.

## Jobcloud Recruiting platform - ONE 
JobCloud ONE is JobCloud's unified B2B recruitment platform designed for employers, hiring managers, and recruiters to manage their end-to-end hiring workflows in one place. 
On ONE, recruiters can 
- create their company profile 
- buy job ad credits via the online shop 
- create and publish their job ads 
- review the applications recevied in our internal ATS and collaborate throughout the hiring process
- manage the user accesses 
- replicate their internal organizations via **workspaces** 

About Workspaces 
Workspaces in JobCloud ONE offer a flexible and customisable solution for recruiters to manage job listings and applications in alignment with their organisational structure. They provide a secure environment by segmenting job listings, ensuring that only authorised personnel have access. Workspaces also enhance resource management by linking to legal entities and managing budgets through wallets. Workspaces are not visible to job seekers and are only used to organise internal teams.

Typical use of workspaces

Consider a large corporation with multiple subsidiaries and departments, each with distinct hiring needs. Workspaces allow the central HR team to create separate environments for each subsidiary or department. For instance, Subsidiary A can have its own workspace for job listings visible only to its HR team and relevant managers. Similarly, Department B can have a workspace for project-specific recruitment, ensuring job listings are accessible only to the relevant team.

Instructions for using workspaces:

Workspaces are not self-service; recruiters must collaborate with the Customer Care Team for the setup.
To update, create, or delete workspaces, contact the Customer Care Team (info@jobcloud.ch) to manage these changes.



## VBP - Value Based Pricing (not live now)
- Pricing principle: recruiters pay per application received, not per ad duration (current model: pay per duration).
- Job seekers cannot be prevented from applying. All applications are received and displayed in the ATS.
- Only applications considered "suitable" are billed. Suitability is based on a minimum CV Insights score.
- Applications below the threshold are not billed. They stay visible in the ATS and are marked "not billed".
- PRO team owns: score computation, billing logic and budget management. The Domain team only displays the billing status in the ATS (see Domain initiative "VBP billing status in ATS").
- Only customers paying via VBP see the billed / not billed differentiation. Customers on pay per duration see no change.
