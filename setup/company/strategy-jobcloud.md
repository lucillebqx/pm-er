# JobCloud strategy context

## Strategic priorities

### RPP and JobCloud ONE monetization

JobCloud is evolving towards monetizing features and services on JobCloud ONE. This direction is associated with the RPP strategy. The exact expansion of the acronym is not recorded here; RPP is used as the established internal term.

The Domain Product Team contributes by improving JobCloud ONE and enabling revenue generation from features offered to targeted customer groups. Commercial packaging, pricing, and the detailed RPP model are not specified in the information currently available and should be confirmed before being documented as strategy.

### Technical capabilities that enable the strategy

The ability to control feature availability for selected customer groups or individual customers is a technical enabler for this direction. It would let Engineering introduce a feature to a limited audience, test it before wider deployment, and expand or withdraw availability in a controlled way. This backend capability supports the overall strategy; it is not itself a front-end optimization or monetization campaign.

## Strategic initiative example

**Backend feature targeting and controlled testing for JobCloud ONE**

- **Strategic link:** Supports RPP and the evolution towards monetizing JobCloud ONE features and services.
- **Expected contribution:** Makes targeted enablement and staged rollout of backend features possible across customer groups and individual customers.
- **Impact:** Medium — an enabling capability in support of the broader strategy.
- **Scope boundary:** Backend feature availability and controlled testing only; front-end experiments and optimization campaigns are out of scope.
- **Measure:** Time to enable or disable a released feature for a target group or individual customer.
- **Status of strategy detail:** Initial context based on product information; validate the wider RPP framing with the relevant strategy stakeholders.
