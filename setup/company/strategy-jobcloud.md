## Domain team strategic context

### RPP and JobCloud ONE monetization

JobCloud is evolving towards monetizing features and services on JobCloud ONE. This direction is associated with the RPP strategy. RPP = Recruiting Process Platform (source: JobCloud Strategy 2026 deck, see below).

The Domain Product Team contributes by improving JobCloud ONE and enabling revenue generation from features offered to targeted customer groups. Commercial packaging, pricing, and the detailed RPP model are not specified in the information currently available and should be confirmed before being documented as strategy.

### Technical capabilities that enable the strategy

The ability to control feature availability for selected customer groups or individual customers is a technical enabler for this direction. It would let Engineering introduce a feature to a limited audience, test it before wider deployment, and expand or withdraw availability in a controlled way. This backend capability supports the overall strategy; it is not itself a front-end optimization or monetization campaign.

## Strategic initiative example

**Backend feature targeting and controlled testing for JobCloud ONE**

- **Strategic link:** Supports RPP and the evolution towards monetizing JobCloud ONE features and services.
- **Expected contribution:** Makes targeted enablement and staged rollout of backend features possible across customer groups and individual customers.
- **Impact:** Medium — an enabling capability in support of the broader strategy.
- **Scope boundary:** Backend feature availability and controlled testing only; front-end experiments and optimization campaigns are out of scope.
- **Measure:** Time to enable or disable a released feature for a target group or individual customer.
- **Status of strategy detail:** Initial context based on product information; validate the wider RPP framing with the relevant strategy stakeholders.

## JobCloud Strategy Update 2025 (December 2025)
Source: "JobCloud Strategy Update 2025 - News & Notes" deck, 10 December 2025 (41 slides, strictly confidential). This section reflects the 2025 deck only.

### 1. Starting point
- Strong strategic assets and market-leading position in CH.
- Brands (YouGov, June 2025): top of mind jobs.ch 39.6 in D-CH (3.7x LinkedIn, 7.1x Indeed); jobup.ch 49.4 in W-CH (3.0x LinkedIn, 14.1x Indeed). Preference: jobs.ch 60.1, jobup.ch 64.9.
- Reach: ~6.2m seeker sessions (Similarweb, Sep 2025), >30k paying customers.
- Traffic: 3.7m and 1.9m monthly visits on the two main platforms; JobCloud is by far the Swiss market leader (Indeed and talent.com follow).
- Market: Swiss vacancies have weakened, but JobCloud's business shows relative strength.

### 2. Strategic evolution pillars (as presented in Dec 2025)
Goal: profitable and sustainable growth.

| Pillar | What it means |
|---|---|
| Ongoing business: acquisition, ACV increase | Increase the number of customers on the platform and optimize yield. Initiatives: SE Marketing Strategy, Sales Enablement, Value Based Pricing, Ecom |
| Digital / Social Recruiting | Integrate an active sourcing offer into the sales offering and tackle the social recruiting space |
| Own and action (quality) seeker data | Increase registered seekers on the platforms and use that data; value-based performance |
| Own the application process | Interface with ATS partners to ensure data capture and good UX |
| Brand | Stay the platforms of first choice and be relevant for all target groups |
| ONE | Establish one platform for all brands |
| Optimize for AI visibility | Adapt platforms, products, content and acquisition strategy to reach the highest AI visibility |
| Enablers | Marketplace 2.0 (incl. Ecom) and AI Enablement |

### 3. Deep dives
- **Marketplace 2.0 (technical backbone):** moves from legacy systems (high friction, triple development effort, dependencies, low data quality) to ONE (one system for all customers and features, clean B2B data), then Marketplace 2.0 (supports VBP, programmatic, next-level ecom, AI support; platform for innovation). All inventory and recruiting processes flow through one system and one data pipeline. ONE represents each customer once; ONE ATS and ATS integration ensure JobCloud owns the apply process; the PPP distribution engine is the foundation of VBP.
- **Value Based Pricing (6 years of learnings):** in 2024, active pricing management added ~CHF 1.05m on an 11.77m OI base (+8.9% price, no sales involvement, no impact on satisfaction). Pay per Performance adoption by Large Enterprises grew from 22% (2023) to 32% (2024). Target experience: recruiter selects role and location, JobCloud shows estimated applications and a fair price in real time (data from 130 markets). Move away from static pricing towards pricing based on value delivered.
- **Social Recruiting "Supreme":** reach passive seekers on social media in addition to active seekers on the platforms (in CH, users open Instagram ~187 and Facebook ~160 times per month).
- **AI Enablement (AI Hub scope):** four dimensions: access and distribution (become the No. 1 source of "jobs in CH" for AI agents), existing value proposition (amaze seekers with AI-enabled experiences), internal processes (AI-driven cost savings, faster processes), new value proposition (exploit value pools outside the marketplace). Aims: seeker relevancy, efficiency and quality, lock-in, monetization.
- **AI Hub mission:** support and upskill the company. No policing: teams act on their own and stay compliant; the Hub advises and helps when asked. Long term: add AI-skilled people to teams cost-neutrally and raise AI literacy.

### 4. News and notes
- **Data audit and process optimization (MP2.0):** assess AI readiness, map the production data landscape and data ownership (platforms, Tealium, CRM), set priorities for scalable AI use cases. Process optimization becomes a strategic pillar of the AI Hub, moving from local optimization to end-to-end flows (first case: lead-to-order).
- **VBP project team:** cross-functional structure with 6 workstreams (pricing and monetization, product offering, communication, system architecture, B2B implementation, B2C platforms). Roles sit within existing org-chart roles. Project lead: Chris A.
- **Social recruiting sales:** "Spotted" products join the SE, ME and LE sales portfolios from 1 Jan 2026 with full OI allocation. Q1 2026 is a launch quarter for SE, with training, sales material, campaigns and a dedicated bonus scheme. Positioning vs LinkedIn and Indeed: local, top on actives, now adding passives; addresses spend usually going to staffing agencies.
- **AI Hub organization:** Head of Data Science (Ignacio Aracena), Senior AI Product Manager (Olfa Abid), regulatory manager hired; Head of AI Hub, data scientists, ML and AI engineers vacant. A Data Science chapter connects data scientists embedded in scrum teams.
- **Office:** partial sublease of the Yond office (zone 1), as average occupancy is max 25%; savings go to ONE, AI Hub and AI literacy.

## JobCloud Strategy 2026 ("Infinity" deck, Zurich, Summer 2026)

### Starting position
- Brands lead the market (YouGov, Nov 2025). Top of mind: jobs.ch 43.0 in D-CH (5.1x LinkedIn, 9.1x Indeed); jobup.ch 49.8 in W-CH (3.3x LinkedIn, 6.6x Indeed). Preference: jobs.ch 64.3, jobup.ch 69.6.
- ~6.6m seeker sessions (2-month avg, Mar/Apr 2026). >30k paying customers. 945k registered seekers.
- All customers on ONE. ~42% of applications arrive as structured data. Direct platform usage: 18% LE, 45% ME, 87% SE.
- Claim: trusted brands, customer access and proprietary data position JobCloud to lead the transition to an AI-enabled marketplace ("defensible data advantage").

### Direction: from job listings to an agentic platform on proprietary data

| Before AI | Agentic platform |
|---|---|
| Limited candidate data (registration light) | Deep candidate data (inferred profiles from seeker behaviour) |
| No pre-application engagement | Pre-application engagement seekers-recruiters |
| Blind process | Trust and disposition data exchange |
| Superficial AI support | Core AI embedding on a Data Lakehouse foundation |
| Highly manual process | Agentic delegation for seekers and recruiters |
| Advertising monetization model | Advertising and recruitment process monetization model |

- What we have: 25 years of employer relationships, access to most seekers, strong B2C and B2B brands, jobs.ch and jobup on a common system, ONE as consolidated B2B platform, DWH, job ad inventory, seeker behavioural data.
- What we need to add: Data Lakehouse, Agentic Interaction Layer, agentic seeker experience, extend ONE into a recruiting process platform, commercialization model for SE, LME and seekers.

### Market forces
- Cyclical pressure: Swiss vacancies weakened since 2023 (FSO JOBSTAT: 125k in Q1 2023, 86k in Q4 2025, 98k in Q1 2026).
- Demographics: more people reach 65 than reach 20; structural talent shortage grows in the coming years.
- AI structural shift, 4 impact dimensions:

| Dimension | Observation | Opportunity | JobCloud response |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Underlying driver | Reduced ad volumes, emerging vs obsolete skills, new scarcity ratios | Monetize market change, market intelligence and training | JobScout24, VBP |
| 2 Enhanced B2B offering | Broad value chain support, recruitment augmentation and replacement | Capture value pools beyond distribution with AI features | RPP |
| 3 Seeker experience | LLM and agentic search adoption, conversational search wins | Meet changing seeker expectations (defend and acquire) | JobScout24 |
| 4 Disintermediation | AI players bypass marketplaces (zero-click world) | Beat generalist AI, stay visible on generalist platforms | AI Search Opt |

- Vacancies stay relatively stable across scenarios up to 2035: growth must come from new value pools.
- Value pools to attack: Automate (internal recruitment expenses and agencies: job brief, scheduling, assessment, interviewing) and Integrate (use JobCloud's ATS footprint to sell value-add services). Pricing tied to outcomes.
- An AI-native seeker layer (teal, huntr, Enhancv, aiApply, Jobcopilot...) can skip job boards. JobCloud AI traffic grew from 0.4% to 1.0% of total traffic (May 2025 vs May 2026, 2.5x); each AI traffic gain drives an outsized SEO drop.
- Value pools 2030 (mCHF, 2025 -> 2030): I Job ads (TAM 164, SAM 156); II Social recruiting ~7 -> ~47; III Employer branding ~30-45 -> ~30-50; IV Active sourcing ~190 -> ~170; V ATS ~25-35 -> ~20-30; VI Internal recruiting efficiency gains ~1-5 -> ~75 (new); VII Agentic seeker experience 0 -> ~1-2; VIII Upskilling lead gen 0 -> ~2-5.

### Strategy: defend and expand
- SOURCE (defend): seeker acquisition, seeker profile, customer acquisition, job ads, search and match.
- SELECT (expand): Recruiting Process Platform covering screening, scheduling, interviewing, background check, ... up to hire.
- Strategic initiatives: Agentic Seeker, Acquisition, ACV increase, (1) SE Marketing Strategy, (2) Sales Enablement, (3) Value Based Pricing, (4) Ecom, (5) Social Recruiting, (6) Brand, (7) Own and Action Seeker Data, (8) AI Enablement, Recruiting Process Platform.
- Low-friction application: the application becomes an interactive process in small steps, with seeker and recruiter engaging before the application. Expected: better conversion, higher satisfaction on both sides, higher application quality.
- Four building blocks: (1) Agentic Seeker Experience, B2C (conversational onboarding, career counselling, auto apply); (2) RPP, B2B (RPP Foundation, RPP use cases); (3) Data Lakehouse, shared data foundation; (4) Agentic Interaction Layer, the nucleus connecting seeker agents and recruiter agents.
- Moat: the integrated process plus proprietary data on seeker and recruiter side cannot be replicated by LLM providers or job boards.

### Seeker perspective
- JobScout24 evolves from job board to agentic career platform: discovery, CV creation, matching (hard skills and desired positions), tailoring of documents per role, auto-apply for high match scores, interview prep and negotiation.
- Inferred profiles are the success factor and moat: every visit enriches a profile from behaviour (Tealium signals converted by AI into structured fields). Goal: reach seekers in the passive phase (3-5 years between job hunts) and build a compounding data asset.

### Recruiter perspective: the Recruiting Process Platform (RPP)
- Principle: free at its core, monetizing value-add use cases (true freemium). Strategic leverage: seeker pool, jobs.ch, jobup.ch, JobScout24, Supreme (TikTok, Instagram).
- Paid add-ons with customer appetite (% from an LME customer survey):

| Add-on | Appetite | Build |
|---|---|---|
| AI social recruiting | In production | Inhouse |
| ATS integrations | In production | Inhouse, unpaid on purpose (owning process and data) |
| AI text creation | 79% | Inhouse |
| AI image and video creation | 49% | Specialist provider |
| AI application qualification | 47% | Specialist provider |
| Candidate matching | 45% | Inhouse |
| Cultural fit | 37% | Specialist provider |
| AI chat bot for seekers | 35% | JobScout24 |
| AI video interviews | 33% | tbd |
| Interview preparation support, automated background checks, AI scheduling, job ad analytics, AI messaging, invite to apply, auto-ask missing information, candidate pre-screening | tbd | Mix of inhouse and specialist providers |
| Career pages | Confirmed (not quantified) | tbd |

- Specialist providers: already in contact with several, including Joveo. No single one covers all cases. Provider mapping is qualitative; no market screening done yet.
- Opportunity zones (ATS distribution CH, proxy from application data May 2025-May 2026): Large customers: 21% known ATS, 55% unknown ATS, 6% customer website, 18% JC solution. Mid volume: 27% known ATS, 22% unknown ATS, 6% website, 45% JC solution. Small: 87% JC solution. Underserved segments are the RPP opportunity.

### Data and AI perspective
- Data Lakehouse with raw, cleansed and business-ready layers; single source of truth for analytics and AI. Sources: Kafka/clicks, ONE, ATS, Salesforce, Abacus, Unity/B2C, Pro/PPP, third-party APIs, legacy, external. Exposed to AI agents and LLMs via MCP.

### Product roadmap ("true freemium")
- Essential building blocks: Data Lakehouse (building then running), Agentic Seeker Layer, Access Control (Wave 1), Partner Connections (Wave 2), Plugins.
- Existing: MCP, AI text generation.
- Wave 1 (RPP): interview preparation, company pages, invite to apply, scheduling, pre-screened candidate, professional analytics, AI application qualification, background checks, AI video interview, career pages.
- Wave 2 (RPP): ask missing info, seeker bot, reference verification, AI videos, AI images, candidate matches, AI candidate message, video transcript, cultural fit.
- Committed (seeker and agentic items): MCP, CV builder and optimizer, candidate comparison, conversational onboarding (MVP JS24), AI career counselling, AI screening questions, personal recommendations, interview prep, auto apply, training / further education lead.
- Directional: verified profiles, scoring incl. feedback loops, passive career management, personalized upskilling, agentic job search and hiring.

### Two-sided data loop
- Seekers: public info (CVs); proprietary (moat): statements of what seekers want, inferred profile. Served by career agent and personalized discovery.
- Customers: public info (job ads); proprietary (moat): statements of what they want beyond the ad, information on previous hiring decisions. Served by AI recruiter support and AI-enabled assessment.
- Exchanges: profile data enrichment, engagement signals, interviews and interactions, feedback and hiring signals. Disposition data flows to seekers, trust flows to customers. Labour market intelligence is a moat on its own.

- Invite to apply: RPP Wave 1, inhouse.
- Cultural fit: RPP Wave 2, paid add-on via specialist provider, 37% LME appetite, depends on Partner Connections and Plugins. Culture data feeds the "what seekers want / what customers want" moats.
