JobCloud has different teams working on matching candidates to a job ad. 

### CV Insights (internal alias: Jobfit)
Within the Domain Team, we work on the CV Insights score 
- what is CV Insights: CV Insіghts analyzes CVs against job ads and provides recruiters with concise, relevant feedback on how well a candidate matches a specific role.
By highlighting key skills and experience relevant to the Job Ad, CV Insights serves as a quick reference for determining candidate suitability, helping recruiters focus on the most promising candidates early in the process.
- Why does CV Insights matter? 
CV Insіghts significantly enhances efficiency in the initial screening phase by cutting down on the time recruiters spend manually reviewing each CV.
With these tailored insights, recruiters can quickly assess candidate fit / Make faster, fact-driven decisions / Minimize time spent on unsuitable applications without compromising quality.
- Where Can Recruiters Access CV Insights?
CV Insіghts will be available within the JobCloud ONE ATS for:
All job ads, regardless of status (live or offline).
All types of job ads, whether free or paid.
All ads published after Oct 9, 2025  (update from Inside Product meeting)
- output of CV Insights 
Presentation of the Candidate: A concise professional persona of the applicant, focusing on recent experience and structured progression.
Suitability Highlights: Key strengths, skills, and experiences that make the candidate a good fit.
Unmet Requirements: Areas where the candidate may not fully meet the job's expectations.
